## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for mission-critical academic and professional assessment. The aesthetic is **Premium Enterprise**, characterized by extreme precision, high information density, and an uncompromising focus on reliability.

The design narrative draws from the technical rigor of developer tools and the clarity of high-end financial platforms. It utilizes a **Minimalist / Corporate Modern** style that prioritizes content over decoration.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by **Professional Indigo** and **Deep Slate**, providing a high-contrast environment.

- **Primary (Indigo):** Used exclusively for primary actions, focus states, and active progress indicators.
- **Secondary (Slate):** Forms the structural foundation of the UI.

## Typography

The system utilizes **Inter** for its exceptional legibility and neutral tone, complemented by **JetBrains Mono** for secondary labels and status badges.
